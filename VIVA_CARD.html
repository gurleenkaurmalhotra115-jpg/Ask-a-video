🎙️ Top 4 Viva Examiner Questions & 15-Sec Answers
  • Q1: Why did semantic chunking fail to beat fixed-token?
    "Spoken transcripts lack formal paragraph pauses; conversational filler triggers false cosine dips, splitting mathematical derivations in half while incurring O(N) neural overhead."
  • Q2: Why does retrieval drop from 23.3% to 3.3% on long lectures?
    "Search Space Expansion. Core keywords ('gradient', 'loss') appear dozens of times across 2 hours, compressing flat dense similarity. Solved by 2-stage hierarchical indexing."
  • Q3: Why was Tier C (NPTEL) recall so high (30%) under acoustic noise?
    "Dense subword embeddings and 120-word windows provide contextual redundancy, absorbing phonetic ASR spelling errors."
  • Q4: How did you prove zero hallucination?
    "+0.2873 cosine margin + closed-world citation prompting forced 26/26 adversarial out-of-domain queries to emit strictly NOT_IN_VIDEO."
